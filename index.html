Process Re-engineering

Redesign core business workflows from the ground up for speed, accuracy, scalability, and control.

ERP Selection & Implementation

01

Talent Acquisition

Source, screen, and onboard skilled professionals aligned to your GCC's capability requirements, culture, and leadership expectations.

02

Workspace Setup

Establish fully operational office infrastructure for your India-based GCC hub — from location selection and fit-out to IT and facilities management.

03

Compliance Management

Navigate India's regulatory, statutory, and labour compliance landscape with confidence — including payroll, tax, and corporate filings.

04

Back-end Support

Ongoing operational and administrative support to keep your GCC running efficiently, from finance and HR back-office to vendor management.
