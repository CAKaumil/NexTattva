Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Working with NexTattva

How quickly can NexTattva onboard and take over our books? +
Our standard ledger diagnostic and transition window takes 5 to 10 business days. During this period, our team audits historical ledgers in Tally Prime, Zoho Books, or SAP, maps GST Input Tax Credit (GSTR-2B reconciliations), aligns PAN/TAN records, and establishes a Day 4 monthly close calendar with zero operational disruption.
What Indian accounting and ERP software do your enterprise consultants support? +
We are completely vendor-agnostic with certified expertise across India-localized ERP ecosystems: Tally Prime (with real-time NIC e-Invoicing and e-Way Bill APIs), Zoho Books, SAP S/4HANA (India Localization CIN & GST), Oracle NetSuite India, Busy, and QuickBooks. We configure charts of accounts strictly aligned to Schedule III of the Companies Act 2013.
How do you ensure Section 43B(h) MSME compliance and GSTR-2B ITC matching? +
We institute an automated 3-way matching protocol (PO vs GRN vs Tax Invoice) with strict 15-day / 45-day payment tracking for micro and small suppliers to safeguard income tax deductibility under Section 43B(h). Additionally, we run continuous GSTR-2B matching to block ineligible ITC before monthly GSTR-3B filings.
Does NexTattva handle monthly payroll filings like EPFO, ESIC, and State Professional Tax? +
Yes, completely end-to-end. We compute gross-to-net payroll, generate Electronic Challan Returns (ECR) for Provident Fund (EPFO) and ESIC before the 15th of each month, remit State Professional Tax (PT) across all operating states, calculate Gratuity Act 1972 accruals, and file quarterly Form 24Q with annual Form 16 Part A/B issuance.
How do your Internal Financial Controls (IFC) prepare us for statutory and tax audit? +
We establish comprehensive Segregation of Duties (SoD), multi-tier approval matrices for bank disbursements, physical fixed asset verification schedules, and CARO 2020 clause audit checklists under Section 134(5)(e). When your statutory auditors step in, we provide full audit schedules, reducing year-end audit cycles by 60%.
Is there a long-term lock-in contract? +
No. We operate on transparent, predictable monthly retainers billed in Indian Rupees. You can scale services or transaction tiers as your business expands, with a standard 30-day notice period.
