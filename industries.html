  • STPI / Non-STPI Softex filing & transfer pricing cost-plus documentation

Vertical 03

Construction, EPC & Real Estate (RERA)

Real estate developers and EPC contractors must balance RERA 70% separate escrow accounts with Section 194C contractor TDS, Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM) on cement/unregistered vendors, and percentage-of-completion revenue recognition.

  • RERA separate project bank account withdrawal certifications
  • Section 194C subcontractor TDS tracking and labour cess compliance
  • RCM liability calculation on unregistered purchases (80% procurement norm)
